Five years ago both candidates and recruiters assumed authenticity in hiring without question, but as AI becomes prevalent that assumption has disappeared. Candidates now wonder if AI filtered them out before a human review, while recruiters question how much of a polished application reflects genuine capability versus AI-generated content.
