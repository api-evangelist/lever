Mar 26 , 15:00 PDT Resolved - At about 3:00pm PST on 3/26/2026 the Lever Support Team triggered a data operation job through an internal admin interface. The data required for this job did not have sufficient database indexes available on the server which caused significant strain on the database servers. The result of this caused site degradation and failed page loads for hire.lever.co between 3:04pm - 3:26pm PST.
