Jun 22 , 11:21 PDT Resolved - Cloudflare has reported that its traffic engineering efforts have successfully mitigated the majority of congestion and packet loss associated with this incident. As a result, service conditions are improving; however, some users may still experience intermittent connectivity issues while their recovery efforts continue. Customer Impact: Most services have stabilized, but some users may continue to experience intermittent latency, timeouts, or connectivity issues during the final stages of recovery.
